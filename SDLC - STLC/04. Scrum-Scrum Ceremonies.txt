

		SCRUM
	-------------

		Scrum is a methodology, a management process. It follows the agile values and principles. 
		
		In Scrum framework,
			
		Product Owner -> User Stories -> Product Backlog  -> Planning Meeting -> Sprint Backlog -> Delivery -> Sprint Demo + Sprint Retro
			
		- Product Owner creates the "User Stories" and they are added to the "Product Backlog (the main requirement document)". We plan (Planning Meeting ) and begin the sprint (2-4 weeks - Sprint Backlog). Then the developer team developes the product and we deliver. Then We have sprint demo and retrospective meetings.
			
	
	SCRUM ROLES
	------------
	
		1. Product Owner
		2. Scrum Master
		3. Developement Team


	MEETINGS
	---------

		-Daily standup meetings - everyday

		-SPRINT PLANNING - FIRST day of sprint

		-SPRINT GROOMING - IN BETWEEN of sprint

		-SPRINT DEMO (Review) - Right After sprint ends

		-SPRINT RETRO / RETROSPECTIVE ---> LAST day of sprint

	--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

	SCRUM CEREMONIES
	----------------
	
	1- ==== STANDUP MEETINGS ==== (Daily Stand-up Meetings)
			
			**** It's called stand-up because the team is making a circle and whose turn is standing up.

		Attendees	: Scrum Master (Leads the team)
					  Development team
					  BA’s (Business Analysis) -> BA is who is communicating with the client about the requirements
					  Product Owner (Optional)
					  
		When 		: Daily - 15mins long

		Purpose		: What did you do yesterday ?
					  What are you doing today ? 
					  Any blockers/impediments ? 


	2- ==== SPRINT PLANNING ====

		Attendees 	: Development team
					  Scrum Master
					  BA’s (Business Analysis)
					  Product Owner
					
		When	  	: At the beginning of the Sprint. 
					  Team meets to discuss user stories & tasks.
					  
		Lead		: Scrum Master but Product Owner makes the decision
		
		Purpose		: Sprint Backlog is defined and estimated
					  We use "Planning Poker" for estimating the effort for a user story. 
					  "Modified Fibonacci" numbers are used in the voting system.
						-Fibonacci 			= 0,1,2,3,5,8,13,21
						-Modified Fibonacci 	= 0,1/2,1,2,3,5,8,13,20 (added 1/2 and round-down to 20)
					  	
		
	3- ==== SPRINT GROOMING / REFINEMENT MEETINGS ====

		Attendees 	: Development team
					  Scrum Master
					  BA’s
					  Product Owners
		
		When		: Product owner and team representatives arrange it in the mid-sprint time. 
					  In this case, planning and refinement meetings alternate but happen on the same day each week.

		Lead		: Product owner
		
		Purpose 	: The grooming involves;
					  splitting big items into smaller ones, rewriting backlog items to be more expressive, deleting no more need items etc.
						

	4- ===== SPRINT DEMO / SPRINT REVIEW ====

		Attendees 	: Development team
					  Scrum Master
					  BA’s
					  Product Owner
					  Customers Stock holders

		When		: Right after sprint ends

		Purpose		: Typically lead by a SME or Test Lead.
					  Show what was accomplished in the sprint.
					  Demonstration client newly created functionality, walk them through and receive feedback


	5- ==== SPRINT RETRO / RETROSPECTIVE MEETINGS ====

		Attendees 	: Development team
					  Scrum Master
					  BA’s
					  Product Owners (Optional)
		
		When		: Right before sprint ends OR/usually LAST DAY OF SPRINT

		Purpose		: What went well ?
					  What could be improved ?
					  What type of issues and problems came up ?
					  Continue doing things that worked.
					  Stop doing things that causes problems.
					  To review and improve sprint cycles
