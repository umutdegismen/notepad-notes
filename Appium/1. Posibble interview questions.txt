
	Appium (posibble) Interview Questions
	------------------------------------
	
	1. What are the benefits of Appium ?
		
		Appium Features

			-Cross-platform compatibility
			-Open source
			-Supports multiple programming languages
			-No app modification required
			-Integration with CI tools
			-Supports native, hybrid, and mobile web applications
			-Real device and emulator/simulator testing
			-Built-in inspector for element identification
			-Parallel execution of tests
			-Wide range of Appium client libraries
